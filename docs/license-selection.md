RT-Library is officially licensed under the **MIT License**. This document preserves the license evaluation and comparative rationale for open-source governance reference.

---

## Selection Rationale

The **MIT License** was selected for RT-Library because:
1. **Permissive and Simple**: Allows personal, educational, and commercial usage, modification, and redistribution with minimal legal friction.
2. **Community Alignment**: Highly standard in the JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Electron desktop ecosystems.
3. **Low Contribution Barrier**: Welcomes open contributions from everyone without requiring complex Contributor License Agreements (CLAs) or copyright assignment.
4. **Transparent Boundaries**: Clearly specifies that the software is provided "as is", with no warranty, while strictly requiring retention of copyright notices.

## 1. MIT License

### Overview
The MIT License is one of the most widely adopted and lightweight permissive licenses in the JavaScript, TypeScript, and Node.js ecosystems.

### Key Characteristics
- **Permissiveness**: Highly permissive. Grants users the right to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and sell copies.
- **Redistribution**: Third parties may redistribute original or modified binaries without making their source code public.
- **Attribution**: The original copyright notice and permission notice must be included in all substantial portions of the software.
- **Modifications**: Derivative works can be relicensed under different terms, including proprietary or closed-source commercial distributions.
- **Open-Source Impact**: Maximizes community adoption, third-party library integration, and developer freedom. It imposes virtually zero administrative overhead on contributors and consumers.

## 2. Apache License 2.0

### Overview
The Apache-2.0 license is a robust permissive license designed for modern software projects, offering explicit patent rights protection while retaining open redistribution freedoms.

### Key Characteristics
- **Permissiveness**: Permissive, allowing commercial, academic, and personal use and modification.
- **Redistribution**: Allowed in source or binary form, provided all copyright, trademark, and attribution notices are preserved.
- **Attribution & State Changes**: Prominently requires modified files to carry notices stating that changes were made.
- **Patent Rights**: Contains an explicit grant of patent rights from contributors to users and includes a patent retaliation clause.
- **Trademark Restrictions**: Explicitly specifies that the license does not grant trademark rights to project names or logos.

---

## 3. GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)

### Overview
GPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license designed to guarantee that software remains free and open-source in all future distributions and derivative works.

### Key Characteristics
- **Permissiveness**: Copyleft. Users have complete freedom to run, study, modify, and share the software, but under the condition that all distributed versions remain under GPL-3.0.
- **Redistribution & Source Disclosure**: Any distributed binary must make the full corresponding source code freely accessible under GPL-3.0 terms.
- **Attribution & Installation Information**: Requires preserving notices, copyright, and providing installation information for consumer devices.
